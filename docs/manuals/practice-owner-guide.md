# Practice owner guide

For practice owners and practice admins. Your practice runs at its own address (for example `sunrise.clinicflow.co.za`). As an owner or practice admin you must use an **authenticator app** (Account → Security).

## 1. Getting started

| Task | Where |
|---|---|
| Practice details, opening hours, branches | Settings, Branches |
| Staff: invite your team, set roles and branches, suspend access when someone leaves | Staff |
| Doctor rosters and appointment slots | Rosters |
| Prices, payments and medical aid billing | Settings, Settings → Payments, Claims |
| Messages to patients (SMS, email, WhatsApp) | Settings → Messaging, WhatsApp |
| Document templates (letters, invoices, prescriptions) | Settings → Templates |
| Your website and online booking | Settings → Website, Website images, Your domain |
| Wallet (pay-as-you-go messages, video and AI) | Settings → Wallet |

## 2. Security for your practice

- **Settings → Security** (owner only): *Require an authenticator app for all staff*. Staff without one are asked to set it up when they next open Clinic Flow.
- Sensitive actions (exports, API keys, break-glass, granting support access) ask staff to **confirm it's them** — password or authenticator code, valid for 15 minutes.
- You are emailed when someone opens an unusually large number of patient records in an hour, and whenever a patient's full record is exported.
- Staff can review their own sign-ins, trusted devices and "sign out other devices" on Account → Security.
- **Audit**: who did what, when.

## 3. Daily work

- **Front desk, triage and My queue** (doctors) update instantly as patients check in and move through the visit.
- **Telemedicine**: video, audio and chat consults; patients pay before joining; extensions can be paid during the call.
- **AI scribe** (if your package or add-on includes it): the doctor asks, the patient agrees, the recording is transcribed and a draft note is written for the doctor to review and save. Recordings are not kept. Included minutes are used first, then your wallet.
- **Lab results**: release to patients with a plain-language explanation the doctor reviews and edits.

## 4. Reports and analytics

- **Reports** (Standard and Pro): build reports from visits, appointments, invoices, payments, claims, lab orders and prescriptions; export CSV, **Excel** or PDF; save and schedule weekly or monthly emails (totals only).
- **Analytics** (Standard and Pro, finance access): key figures with the previous period, a six-month trend, doctor and branch comparisons. Figures refresh every 5 minutes.

## 5. Patients' privacy (POPIA)

- Patients see and download their own records in the portal and choose what to share with connected systems.
- Only staff whose role needs patient records can open them; every opening is recorded.
- Use **break-glass** only in emergencies — it requires a reason and is reviewed.

## 6. Getting help

Open a ticket in **Support**. If the platform team needs to look inside your practice, you grant time-limited access; it is logged and you can revoke it.
