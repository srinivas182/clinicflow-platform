# 0001. Record architecture decisions

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-02
- Deciders: Mayura Consultancy Services (tech lead), Sekal

## Context

Clinic Flow will be built by two squads over 20 sprints and maintained for years. Decisions must outlive the people who made them.

## Decision

Every significant technical decision is recorded as a short ADR in `docs/adr`, numbered, with context, decision and consequences. ADRs are never edited after acceptance; a new ADR supersedes an old one.

## Consequences

New team members can see why the system looks the way it does. Reviews can point to a decision instead of re-arguing it.
